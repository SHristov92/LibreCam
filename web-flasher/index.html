Wiring
  • Use a 3.3 V USB serial adapter. 5 V on the pins can damage the chip.
  • Adapter TX to the camera's RX1, adapter RX to TX1, and GND to GND.
  • Power the camera from its own supply (or the adapter's 3.3 V if it can deliver 300 mA or more).
  • The camera's bootloader only listens for a moment after power-on: after you press Start, unplug and replug the camera's power.
