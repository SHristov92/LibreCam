Maintenance

Update the firmware, restart the camera or erase its settings.

Firmware update

Installed: -

The camera checks the package, restarts, and its bootloader installs it (about a minute; the LED stays dark). Settings and SD card files stay.

Restart

Recordings in progress are cut short.

Factory reset

Erases every setting, including Wi-Fi. The camera then starts its setup access point. Files on the SD card stay.
